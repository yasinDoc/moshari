# Moshari voiceover

**Target length:** about 50 seconds.

This is Moshari, a ward-level dengue early warning tool. It forecasts expected cases two, three and four weeks ahead for 60 wards across Dhaka, Chattogram, Gazipur and Narayanganj.

Here on the ward net, the colour shows each ward's risk tier. Starting with the highest-risk wards helps teams decide where larvicide and fogging should begin.

Opening a ward shows the outlook, an 80 percent range, the recommended response, and the weather and case drivers behind the forecast. The rainfall slider lets an officer see how a wetter week could change risk.

Moshari was tested chronologically on 2024 and 2025. It averages about one case of error per ward per week, and 83 percent of forecast weeks fall in the right tier.

The model runs entirely on a laptop, with no forecasting API calls. Moshari aims to catch dengue before it reaches the hospital.
